Privacy

How X Cron Jobs handles your data. Product disclosure for the India-first MVP — not legal advice.

Profile visibility

Show my contact is off by default and reversible. Recruiters see your contact and apply profile when you Easy apply — not before.

Separate consents

Job match alerts, marketing tips, and channel toggles (in-app / push / email) are separate. Manage them under Settings → Prefs.

Easy apply

Shares your selected apply-profile snapshot and contact with that employer for that job. We do not sell candidate lists.

Your controls

Export my data and Delete account live on the Account tab. Optional AI tools clean job text or help you use the app — they do not rank candidates or infer sensitive traits.
