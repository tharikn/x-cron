Privacy

How X Cron Jobs handles your data. Product disclosure for the India-first MVP — not legal advice.

Profile visibility

Show my contact is off by default and reversible. Recruiters see your contact and apply profile when you Easy apply — not before.

Separate consents

Job match alerts, marketing tips, and channel toggles (in-app / push / email) are separate. Manage them under Settings → Preferences.

Easy apply

Shares your selected apply-profile snapshot and contact with that employer for that job. We do not sell candidate lists.

Account export & delete

Signed-in users can export a portable JSON copy of their account data and delete the account from Account settings. Guest device preferences stay on this browser until cleared.

Notifications & devices

Push or browser alerts require your permission. Tokens are linked while signed in and unlinked on logout. Camera access is requested only for bulk import OCR.

Support references

Error toasts may include a short ref: so support can locate a request — never your password.
